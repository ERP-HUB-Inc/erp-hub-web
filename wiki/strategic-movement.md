That’s a **smart question at the perfect time** — before you start paying for API usage or building payment flows, it’s best to first shape a **lean business strategy** that validates your idea and attracts early users *even without a company name yet*.

Let’s structure your strategy for this **early validation stage** 👇

---

## 🧭 1. Define the Core Business Vision

You already have a great seed idea:

> “AI-assisted stock and purchase order generation inside an ERP system.”

That’s **a high-value automation feature** for SMEs, retailers, and distributors who handle repetitive procurement work.

So your **initial vision** can be framed as:

> “Empower small and medium businesses to automate purchase orders and inventory planning using AI — without needing a data analyst or IT team.”

This gives you a north star while you build your MVP.

---

## 🧱 2. Start with a Lean “Micro Brand” (No Legal Entity Yet)

You don’t need a full company right away — just a *functional name and landing presence*.

### Strategy:

* Pick a **temporary brand name** (even a project name like *“SmartStock AI”* or *“Procura ERP”*).
* Build a **one-page landing site** using Notion, Framer, or Webflow.
* Include:

  * Headline: your value proposition.
  * Demo video or screenshots of AI PO generation.
  * Signup / “Join Waitlist” or “Try Demo” button (collect emails).
  * Optional: “Buy Credits (Coming Soon)” to signal business intent.

This validates user interest **before formal incorporation**.

---

## 🔍 3. Identify Your Early Adopter Market

Your first users should be businesses that already use spreadsheets or simple ERPs but lack AI automation.
Focus on one clear segment:

| Segment                    | Problem                 | How AI Feature Helps                        |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Retail stores (mini marts) | Manual reorder tracking | AI auto-suggests purchase orders            |
| Wholesalers / distributors | Too many SKUs           | AI imports supplier stock lists & forecasts |
| Online sellers             | Unclear reorder timing  | AI forecasts based on sales data            |

🎯 Pick **one niche** for your MVP (e.g., “retail stores under 50 staff”) — easier to sell to and design for.

---

## 💡 4. Build the MVP Before the Full ERP

You don’t need the entire ERP to start.

Start with **an AI-powered module** that plugs into existing systems or works standalone.

### MVP Feature Example:

> “Upload your last 3 months of sales data → AI suggests a purchase order for next month.”

Core functions:

* Upload Excel/CSV.
* Call OpenAI/Claude API to generate stock suggestions.
* Display or export a draft PO.
* Track credits manually at first (no payment gateway yet).

This gives a **working demo** that shows real value to SMEs.

---

## 💵 5. Simulate the Credit System Manually (Soft Launch)

Don’t rush into full Bill24 integration yet.

**Soft-launch version:**

* Manually grant credits (e.g., 50 free credits to try AI).
* When they run out, use manual payments:

  * “Message us on Telegram to top up 10 USD = 100 credits.”
  * Use ABA QR or Bill24 invoice manually.
* Record credit usage in a Google Sheet or database.

Once you get your first 20–30 active users, then move to automated Bill24 integration.

This avoids overhead until you have consistent transactions.

---

## 📈 6. Validate Before Incorporating

Wait until you reach any of these milestones before officially registering your company:

| Milestone                          | Why it matters              |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| 20+ active users                   | Product has traction        |
| 3+ paying customers                | Revenue validation          |
| Consistent $100–$300/month revenue | Cash flow can sustain costs |
| Feedback from real SMEs            | Clear product direction     |

Then you can safely form your company in Cambodia or Singapore (depending on your expansion goals).

---

## 🪙 7. Launch Strategy (Pre-Company)

**Timeline Example:**

| Week | Goal                                               |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| 1–2  | Build landing page + set up AI demo backend        |
| 3    | Test credit deduction manually with 2–3 users      |
| 4    | Collect testimonials + measure feature usage       |
| 5    | Announce soft launch + optional early access group |
| 6+   | Prepare legal entity + Bill24 API integration      |

You’ll have a running prototype and initial customer feedback *before* you even register your company — that’s real validation.

---

## 🚀 8. Branding Tip While You’re Unnamed

If you don’t want to commit to a brand yet:

* Use a **project-style name**: e.g., `aiorder.io`, `smarterp-beta.app`, `project-stockmind`.
* Add a note like:

  > “Project in early beta — building the future of AI-powered ERP automation.”

This keeps you flexible while still professional-looking.
